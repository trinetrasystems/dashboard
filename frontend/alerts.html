Connecting a speaker
  1. Switch the speaker on and put it in pairing mode (usually: hold its Bluetooth button until it blinks), within a few metres of the Jetson.
  2. Find speakers (12 s), then Connect next to it.
  3. Test beep, then switch the beep on.
The Jetson reconnects to the speaker by itself before each beep. Keep the speaker on mains power with its auto-off disabled: a speaker that switched itself off cannot beep. The browser beep on every open dashboard page stays as it is.
